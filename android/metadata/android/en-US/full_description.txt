Bolsillo BCH is a simple, minimalist Bitcoin Cash (BCH) wallet.

Keep your BCH under your own control with full self-custody. No accounts, no intermediaries - just your keys, your coins.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN
- Clean, minimal interface that is easy to understand at a glance.
- Send and receive BCH in seconds with QR codes.
- Real-time balance tracking with support for multiple wallets.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
- Value shown in local currency so you always know what you have.
- Full control over your funds at all times.
- Open source and community reviewed.

Secure by default:
- PIN and biometric lock (fingerprint / face unlock).
- Optional encrypted backup to Google Drive to recover your wallet on a new device.

Bolsillo BCH: the simplest way to hold and use Bitcoin Cash.
