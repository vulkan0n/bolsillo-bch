Bolsillo BCH is a simple, minimalist Bitcoin Cash (BCH) wallet built for people who want to use crypto without the hassle.

YOUR BCH UNDER YOUR CONTROL
- Full self-custody: only your keys, your coins. No accounts, no intermediaries.
- Real-time balance tracking, always shown in your local currency (ARS) with the BCH equivalent.
- Support for multiple wallets.

NO COMPLICATIONS
- Sign in with your Google account: your wallet is created and backed up automatically, encrypted in Google Drive.
- Send and receive BCH in seconds with QR codes.
- Notifications when you receive payments, plus a "send all" button to move your balance in one tap.

SECURE BY DEFAULT
- PIN and biometric lock (fingerprint / face unlock).
- Automatic encrypted cloud backup and PIN-less recovery via a security question.
- Built on open source (BSD-3).

Bolsillo BCH: the simplest way to hold and use Bitcoin Cash.
